About

I am a Professor in the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering at Manav Rachna International Institute of Research and Studies (MRIIRS), Faridabad. I graduated in Electronics and Communication Engineering in 2001 and have been teaching ever since.

My academic path combines engineering with management: an M.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering (2006), a Master of Development and Business Administration (MDBA) from Symbiosis, Pune, and a Ph.D. completed in 2016. This blend shapes how I mentor students, run departmental work and approach research.
