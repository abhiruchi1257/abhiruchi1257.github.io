About

I am a Professor in the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering at MRIIRS, Faridabad. I graduated in Electronics and Communication Engineering in 2001 and have been in the teaching profession for more than 23 years.

My academic path combines engineering with management: an MDBA from Symbiosis, Pune, followed by an M.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering in 2006 and a PhD in 2016. This helps me teach technical subjects with an eye on how they are used in industry and in organisations.

I mentor students through coursework, projects and research, and publish my work for the wider academic community.
